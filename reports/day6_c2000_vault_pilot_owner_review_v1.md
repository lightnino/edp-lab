The current witnesses establish physical fields only; they do **not** establish uniqueness or business equivalence.

## Decisions and subsequent protected scope

1. **Semantic decision:** For each of the three new families, approve a versioned source-native contract after the D1–D5 evidence above is completed, request a different model, or explicitly defer business integration. A deferred family remains counted as unmodeled; it cannot be reported as a full C2000 Vault PASS.
2. **Conditional prototype:** The accepted 127-occurrence FSDS prototype is reused for the three exact sources. A new bounded prototype is required only where an approved new model, changed mapping/history, unverified physical shape or unproven loader materially triggers V3. The remaining 57 FSDS NUM headers are a shape-check dependency; no new write has yet been authorized for them.
3. **Full-write authority:** The installed signed Vault V2 policy expressly sets `c2000_full_integration_allowed=false` and caps a run at 1,000 DATA occurrences. The protected Broker/Executor has not demonstrated full-scope execution. Before V4, the Owner must approve the exact selected source population, target tables, plan, per-run/batch limits and budget under owner-controlled trust; an installed protected execution path must enforce those pins. The Step 2 direct DEV authorization cannot be reused. No Agent signature or repository edit can grant this scope.

The next autonomous resume consumes approved decisions and exact signed scope evidence, continues unaffected approved families, and stops again only if an actual semantic, integrity, permission or budget boundary remains.
