# C2000 Vault pilot protected execution boundary

The approved FSDS `num.txt` Contract V1 covers 60 C2000 deliveries. The accepted historical M2 baseline records **176,060,325** `value` field occurrences for those 60 NUM bindings. The Vault prototype loaded only 127 selected DATA occurrences from three of those sources. The 1,893 EDGAR, 42 13F and five Treasury sources have no approved Vault business mappings.

The owner-installed signed Vault V2 policy has `c2000_full_integration_allowed=false`, a maximum of 1,000 DATA occurrences per run, and zero write retries. Its protected executor and exact-row verifier were designed for small fixed plans; they have not demonstrated a practical 176-million-row FSDS load, let alone all 2,000 sources. A naive 1,000-row partition of the historical FSDS NUM volume would require at least **176,061 runs** before headers, negatives or other families, which exceeds the installed policy's 64-grant total and 2,000-statement total. That is a capacity calculation, not an approved batch plan or cost estimate.

The runner can checkpoint protected batches and reconcile signed readback without uncertain-write replay. Its V9 path requires an independently signed live Vault freeze from the protected service before Git exit. The installed service does not provide the required full-population V4 execution or V9 freeze action. These are technical and owner-controlled authority boundaries; the runner cannot create the missing policy, grant or verifier signature.

Owner review can choose one exact next scope: an approved, resource-bounded FSDS expansion using a suitable protected native Spark/Delta executor, or a smaller genuinely representative protected increment while full-scale capacity is measured. Separately approve or defer the three new source-family semantics using the [consolidated packet](day6_c2000_vault_pilot_owner_review_v1.md). Until the selected sources, approved contracts, input/plan hashes, target tables, compute budget and protected policy are exact, V4 remains paused and no PASS for full C2000 is possible.
