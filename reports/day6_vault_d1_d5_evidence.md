## D4 — Satellite and history

**OBSERVED:** the accepted M1 record contract carries ordered `(field_ordinal, original_name, lexical_value, disposition)` for every DATA row and explicit source/binding/run/line/byte lineage. For the three complete NUM bindings, the accepted binding counters are **7,917,453** total physical lines, **7,917,450** DATA rows and **79,174,500** DATA field occurrences: exactly one header/framing line per member and ten fields per DATA row. The [existing M1 validation](../docs/day2_c300_m1_readiness_v1.md) requires full member byte reconstruction, duplicate multiplicity, field multiset and original interval checks; Day 6 did not rerun the historical full-population verification. The [historical M2 relation](../docs/historical_c750_m2.md) preserves M1 columns and adds a separately versioned typed `value` result at one-to-one technical locator grain.

The `SRC000054` RAW line **337467**, byte offset **46589850**, length **244**, contains a quoted `segments` value with an embedded literal TAB. A naive `split('\\t')` yields 11 pieces, whereas the accepted pinned M1 record has ten ordered fields and preserves the quoted segment text (including TAB), `value=9830000.0000`, empty footnote. This is an exact negative control for any new Vault loader: it must consume the approved M1 lexical array or reproduce its scoped parser contract, not silently reparse the RAW with a generic split. The Day 6 occurrence probe deliberately records `WIDTH_MISMATCH_SIMPLE_SPLIT_USE_PINNED_M1` rather than guessing a correction.

**PROPOSED:** occurrence-anchored lexical and scoped M2 Satellites with exact source field arrays, presence states, model/rule versions, source provenance and append-only observations. The proposed lexical hashdiff is equal for the exact-equal `SRC000064` pair while their technical occurrence keys differ; it changes for the differing-value pair. Neither payload hash nor typed Decimal is an occurrence identifier. Original `ddate`/`qtrs` remain source reporting context; warehouse load time is separate, and no business-effective interval is inferred. Late arrivals and corrections append as distinct deliveries/observations. These historization choices need owner review before implementation; no Vault rows were written.

## D5 — Technical occurrence and lineage

**OBSERVED, bounded complete NUM scope:** all **7,917,450** DATA records have distinct `(source_instance_id,binding_sha256,source_line)` and distinct `(source_instance_id,binding_sha256,byte_offset,byte_length)` in the pinned M1 snapshots. There are three accepted bindings and two operational run IDs. A separate `SUB` and NUM RAW read checked the relevant ZIP/member hashes before using bytes. The bounded probe read five exact physical lines twice with the same RAW/member hash and reproduced identical occurrence keys and payload hashes where a simple parse was valid. Its two equal-value/equal-byte lines have different offsets and different occurrence keys. This replay checks five lines; it is not a full binding replay or proof that a new production Vault load is deterministic.

**PROPOSED/VALIDATED mechanically at inspected scope:** physical key `(source_instance_id,archive_member,byte_offset,byte_length)`, with source line, binding hash, RAW/member hashes and parser versions as required assertions/lineage. `run_id` changes with execution and is excluded from the physical key; the source occurrence remains the same on replay. Field identity appends ordinal. Header/framing rows are retained in the lineage structure and counted separately from DATA observations. A same-Source-ID RAW hash mismatch, changed interval, overlapping/gapped member intervals or field ordinal mismatch is a hard integrity failure. Neither a physical key nor the lexical hashdiff becomes a Business Hub key.
