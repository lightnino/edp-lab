## Objective and distinction

The original Day 5 objective remains **C2000 = C1500 + N500, proven correction or controlled M3 case, targeted historical rebuild, independent old/new/combined Stability, and Agent V3 Data Vault proposals**.

Add a distinct operational experiment: make **Runtime Agent A** capable of completing *multiple previously authorized mechanical actions* without requesting another step-specific, manually edited Python owner SHA pin. This is different from Agent V3's semantic/Vault capabilities and from a Codex engineering assistant executing user-written instructions.

Human approval remains mandatory for new cohorts/source scopes, substantive model changes, new economic/identity/mapping interpretations, extra target tables or writes, and any workflow scope increase. **Repeated approval for the same approved scoped substeps is the defect to fix; explicit authorization is not.**

## Minimal scope of the permanent fix (no new framework)

1. **Workflow authorization envelope:** authenticated owner grants an immutable, versioned policy document describing `workflow_id`, approved cohort/identities/source hash manifest, approved model/parser/rule versions, allowed operations (`inspect/upload_verified_original/profile/process/publish/validate/retry`, as applicable), allowed output paths/tables, execution principal/environment, bounded resource budget and expiry/revocation rules. Do not authorize operations absent from the owner's exact approval.
2. **Independent trust:** reuse existing owner approval, trusted host install/trust anchor, UniverseGate and tool adapters where applicable. The policy/runtime independently verifies approval origin/integrity and current scope; no request-supplied SHA or self-authored `status: OWNER_APPROVED` becomes trusted authority. **Do not hardcode a new Python SHA constant per workflow step.** Prefer a small versioned approved policy registry and stable host trust anchor, not a network policy service.
3. **Enforcement:** every tool/job action checks source identity/RAW hash, approved parser/model/config hash, exact operation, output destination, runtime identity, expiry/revocation and budget; enforce with actual Databricks/UC permissions where possible. No prompt-only guardrails. Remote RAW still needs independent SHA verification **per source**.
4. **Resume and audit:** record policy ID/version, tool/agent/code/config versions, decision allow/deny, source IDs, fingerprints, run/checkpoint, retries, output readback and actual human review/intervention. Idempotent mechanical retries within scope do not require reapproval; changed scopes do.
5. **Backward compatibility:** leave prior N450 and N750 admission/Step 8 records intact. New policy is opt-in for **Day 5 pilot**; old approved paths continue under their actual historical limits. Never retrofit an old grant to a new cohort or infer Day 4 approval from these notes.

## Non-negotiable evaluation cases

- **Allowed:** authenticated owner installs a bounded N500 pilot policy; Agent A performs at least two distinct authorized mechanical phases (e.g., inspect/profile and validate/retry; after appropriate processing grant, process and verify) without another manual source-code SHA pin edit.
- **Denied:** out-of-universe or out-of-cohort source; scope widening; unapproved parser/M3 version; unapproved new destination/write; fake approval generated by Agent; missing/mismatched remote RAW hash; expired/revoked policy; unauthenticated principal; instruction injection in source/tool data.
- **Correctness:** retain independent Lossless 1–6/Stability and exact approved contract checks; no self-chosen semantic business key, identity merge, interpretation or truth promotion.
- **Evidence:** differentiate `DENIED_BY_POLICY`, `BLOCKED_REMOTE_SHA`, `HUMAN_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`, `COMPLETED_VALIDATED`, `UNSUPPORTED`; never call safe denial successful onboarding.
- **Operating cost:** measure policy creation/installation, human approval, manual steering, correction, exception review and retry minutes separately; compare with known Day 3/4 legacy-grant process without inventing a human-only baseline.
